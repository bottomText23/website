Some stuff about me

I started this website in early 2024 wanting to get away from mainstream social media and have been using it as a repository of my art and thoughts. The layout might not be the prettiest and the code a complete mess, but what matters to me is for this website to be made by myself from scratch.

I like nature, programming, Jesus Christ, sketching, worldbuilding, anime and most things DIY. I enjoy reading fiction (mainly fantasy) but sometimes dabble into psychology and philosophy.

You might have noticed this website is written in english. Aside from that I know spanish, german and polish. If you wish to contact me... tough luck... I will add a contact email eventually.

Please check out my writing and/or my art if you're interested. Have a nice day!
